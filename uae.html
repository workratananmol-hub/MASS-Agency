UAE delivery considerations

Map the operating context before choosing the stack.

UAE projects can involve customer data, messaging, payment, hosting, language, regulatory, or regional vendor constraints. MASS captures those requirements during discovery and builds against the client’s approved operating model. MASS does not provide legal advice.

Data and access

Define which records an automation or application may read, write, store, and retain before implementation begins.

Regional systems

Payment, CRM, messaging, identity, and other vendors are checked for supported integration paths rather than assumed.

Human approval

High-impact customer, financial, or contractual actions can remain behind an explicit review step.

Language requirements

If English and Arabic experiences are required, content ownership, translation, layout testing, and search strategy should be scoped as real product work.

Hosting and vendors

Infrastructure choices should reflect the client’s security, data, performance, and operational requirements.

Ownership

Repositories, domains, accounts, credentials, and documentation are defined so the client knows exactly what it controls after launch.
