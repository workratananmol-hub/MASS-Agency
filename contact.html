What helps a useful first conversation

You do not need a finished specification. A useful first message names:

  • The business problem, not only the tool you think you need
  • The outcome that would make the work successful
  • The systems, data, or website already in play
  • Any timing constraints you already know

Budget range is optional. It helps MASS judge fit; it is not a quote.

What happens next

MASS reviews whether the work is a fit, then discusses the most useful next step—usually a tighter brief, a discovery conversation, or a project-specific estimate. There is no published response-time guarantee.

MASS is based in Herndon, Virginia, and works with clients beyond the local area.
