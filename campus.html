A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Before we
meet.

Do students need coding experience?

The seminar suits beginners. For masterclasses, we select tools and prerequisites to match the cohort. Share your students’ background so we can choose the right level.

Is this only for engineering students?

No. Business, management, and other student groups can explore automation, product thinking, and sales. We adapt the technical depth to the audience.

What will students take away?

Seminar participants gain a practical understanding of AI use cases and the path from idea to customer. Masterclass participants work towards a small workflow or prototype and practise a customer-focused pitch. The exact build depends on the agreed agenda and student readiness.
