01 / WHY BRING THIS TO CAMPUS?

From a tool demo to
a useful solution.

A useful AI project starts with a real task. Students map the task, test an approach, and practise explaining the value to someone who might use it. That gives the session a practical shape without promising a business outcome.

Each host starts from a different learning goal. A high school group may need an accessible introduction to AI and problem-solving; a college or university cohort may want to examine an automation or product concept; a coaching or education centre may want an activity that fits its existing group. We agree the technical depth, tools, and activity with the coordinator before confirmation.

Before a date is confirmed, the conversation covers the cohort, learning goal, session format, practical requirements, and host arrangements. That gives the coordinator a clear basis for deciding whether the session is right for the group.

A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Before we
meet.

Who can host a MASS Campus session?

High schools, colleges, universities, coaching and tuition organisations, education centres, E-cells, incubators, student clubs, and academic departments can enquire. We first discuss the cohort and learning goal, then agree the right format.

Do students need coding experience?

The AI Discovery Seminar suits beginners. For a masterclass, we choose tools and prerequisites to match the cohort. Share the students’ background when you enquire.

Is it only for engineering students?

No. Business, management, and other student groups can explore AI automation, product thinking, and customer value. The technical depth is agreed for the audience.

What will students do in the session?

They explore a real task, work through an AI workflow or product idea, check outputs, and practise explaining the value to a potential user. The exact activity depends on the agreed agenda and cohort readiness.

What are the available formats?

The AI Discovery Seminar is 60–90 minutes. The Build-to-Sell Masterclass is 3–4 hours and includes a guided build. A longer or department-specific session can be discussed with MASS.

Can a high school group take part?

Yes. We discuss the students’ level and the host’s goal before choosing the activity and technical depth. The session is planned around the agreed cohort.

What does the institution need to arrange?

The host nominates a coordinator and arranges the audience, venue, display, audio, and internet. Hands-on sessions also need student laptops and the agreed tool accounts.

Can we discuss certificates or student outcomes before booking?

Please raise any certificate requirements or student-outcome expectations when you enquire. MASS agrees the agenda, practical details, and what the institution needs before confirmation; it does not promise internships, jobs, or placement outcomes.

How do we discuss fees, dates, and delivery details?

Send the cohort, group size, location, preferred dates, and learning goal to info@mass.llc. MASS will discuss scope, preparation, requirements, fees, availability, and delivery details before confirmation.
