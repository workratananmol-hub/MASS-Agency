Oakspire Tech

A professional platform for a business whose clients and operations needed the same system, not two disconnected ones.

Discipline

Business platform + AI workflows

The need

Easier client engagement. Cleaner daily operations.

What shipped

Custom website, automation, AI-powered operational flows

The standard

Design around how the work actually moves

The engagement

Digital infrastructure shaped around real operations.

The situation

Oakspire Tech needed a professional digital presence that made the business easier to approach—and workflows that could keep up once a client did. A brochure site would have solved the first half of that sentence. It would have left the second half in inboxes, spreadsheets, and tribal knowledge.

MASS built a custom business website and then connected automation and AI-powered workflows to the way Oakspire already operated. The platform was not a layer of decoration over the company. It was designed around the path a client takes, and around the work the team has to do next.

01 / Problem

Make the business easier to engage—and easier to run.

Client interactions were harder than they needed to be. Day-to-day operations depended on steps that did not scale cleanly. Oakspire needed a platform that could hold both: a credible public face, and operational flows that did not collapse when volume increased.

02 / Approach

Start from the work, then choose the software.

MASS mapped how clients arrive, what they need to understand, and which internal steps repeat after every enquiry. The website was built as a custom professional platform. Automation and AI-powered workflows were then fitted to those operational requirements—not to a generic “AI for business” template.

03 / Outcome

Less friction on both sides of the desk.

Clients meet a clearer, more professional digital experience. The team moves repeatable work into dependable automated flows. Efficiency improved because the platform was built around the journey, not around a stack of disconnected tools.

What MASS delivered

A public platform with operational intelligence.

  1. 01

    Custom business website

    A professional client-facing site that explains the company, supports enquiry, and holds the brand with more discipline than a template.

  2. 02

    Operational automation

    Workflows tailored to Oakspire’s actual requirements—so repeatable client and internal steps do not live in ad-hoc tools.

  3. 03

    AI-powered workflows

    Agentic assistance where it shortens a defined operational task, with the rest of the process remaining under human control.

  4. 04

    One connected journey

    The public site and the work behind it share a structure, so a client interaction does not fall into a gap between “marketing” and “ops.”

One platform. Better flow.

Oakspire Tech is what a professional-services site looks like when it is designed as infrastructure. If your clients and your operations still live in different systems, start there.
