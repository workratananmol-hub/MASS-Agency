How remote delivery works

Define the interfaces, then remove the handoff noise.

Remote work succeeds when ownership is explicit. MASS scopes who decides, which systems are in bounds, when reviews happen, what access is needed, and what the client owns after launch.

One decision owner

A named client approver prevents design, product, and technical decisions from stalling across long message threads.

Written scope

Users, workflows, pages, integrations, data, and exclusions are documented before implementation expands.

Shared source of truth

Repository, documents, credentials, and project decisions live in agreed systems instead of private inboxes.

Reviewable increments

Work is shown in pieces that can be tested and corrected before a large final reveal.

Deployment ownership

The client keeps ownership of domains, hosting, repositories, and third-party accounts where the engagement allows it.

Clear recurring cost

Hosting, model/API usage, software subscriptions, and optional support are separated from implementation cost.
