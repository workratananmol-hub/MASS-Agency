# AI Avatar.

An internal MASS experiment in keeping one AI-generated identity consistent across posts. It is not a client service, not a live influencer account we sell, and not an affiliate program we operate.

What this is

Internal MASS venture and demonstration. Not a client package.

Exists today

Identity-consistent stills and caption drafts for review.

Does not exist

A public avatar account MASS sells, or verified retail affiliates.

Disclosure

AI-generated posts would be labeled as such. We do not hide the method.

The experiment

## One character. Reviewed posts.

The useful question is whether an AI identity can stay recognizable without drifting. MASS is testing that. We are not claiming a media network, a talent roster, or partnerships with retailers.

01

### Identity consistency

Generate stills of one defined character and check them against a reference so face, wardrobe, and tone do not wander without a decision.

02

### Draft production

Produce caption and image drafts for a named niche. A person reviews before anything is scheduled or published.

03

### Not affiliate commerce

We do not have verified affiliate relationships to name here. Linking out to shops is a possible future test, not a current program.

Review and disclosure

## If it publishes, it is labeled.

### Content review

No automated post to Instagram from this project without a human check for likeness drift, claims, and platform rules. MASS does not run an unattended posting bot as a client deliverable under this name.

### Disclosure

Synthetic or AI-generated images and captions would be disclosed as AI-generated. We will not present an avatar as a real person. We will not imply an endorsement from a brand we do not have.

Existing capability

## Generate and review.

-   Reference-locked stills of one character
-   Caption drafts for a defined niche
-   Human review before publish

Plans — not claims

## A portfolio of brands is a hypothesis.

Multiple identities, always-on calendars, and commerce links are roadmap ideas. They are not live MASS products and not something we will sell as “AI influencer retainers” until the review and disclosure rules above are productized.
