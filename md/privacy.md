# Privacy Policy.

This policy describes how MASS LLC (“MASS,” “we,” “us”) handles personal information on the public website at [mass.llc](https://mass.llc/). It is written to match how this site is built and hosted today—not a generic template.

## This website is not a client project

This policy covers information processed because you visit mass.llc, follow a link on it, email or call the published contact details, or use the contact page. Data processed under a separate client engagement—source code, customer files, hosting credentials, training corpora, production logs inside a delivered system—is governed by that project’s contract and any data-processing terms agreed for the work. Those arrangements are not created by this policy.

## Information you choose to send

If you email [info@mass.llc](mailto:info@mass.llc), call the published number, or use the contact page, you may send:

-   Name
-   Work email address
-   Company name
-   A description of the problem, outcome, systems, timing, and optional budget range
-   Whatever else you put in the message or say on the call

The contact page does not post that form to a MASS server. The browser opens your email client with a message addressed to info@mass.llc (a `mailto:` link). MASS receives the content only if you send the email. Your email provider then processes the message in the ordinary way. If you do not send it, MASS does not receive those fields from the form.

The Campus AI sessions enquiry form follows the same email-only path. It can prepare an enquiry containing your name, email address, institution, city, chosen session format, and the details you enter. The MIMS waitlist form can prepare an email containing your email address and selected role. Neither form sends those details to a MASS server unless you review and send the prepared email.

We use this information to decide whether the work is a fit, to reply, and to keep a record of the inquiry as needed for a potential engagement or legal obligation. We do not use it to run advertising campaigns from this website.

## Information collected when you load a page

### Hosting logs

The site is a set of static files hosted on Hostinger shared hosting (Apache). Like most web hosts, Hostinger may record request logs that can include IP address, date and time, requested URL, referrer, and user-agent. MASS uses those logs, when available, to operate, secure, and debug the site. Retention of host logs follows Hostinger’s systems, not a separate MASS analytics warehouse.

### Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is loaded only after you choose to allow analytics in the website’s Privacy settings. The measurement ID is `G-S0TWT8QTLL`. You can accept, reject, or change that choice at any time using the Privacy settings control.

If you allow analytics, Google LLC processes measurement data on MASS’s behalf as an analytics provider. Typical fields can include approximate location derived from IP, device and browser type, pages viewed, referring URL, and timestamps. Google’s own terms and privacy policy apply to that processing. Google may process data in the United States and other countries where it operates.

The custom 404 and 500 documents do not load the Analytics tag. This does not prevent Hostinger from keeping ordinary server logs.

### What this site does not load

This website does not load advertising networks, a live-chat widget, a payment form, embedded maps, or a newsletter signup. Some pages use locally hosted video and animation assets. Fonts and animation libraries are served from this site’s own `/assets` files, not from a font or CDN third party. There is no service worker. The site stores the analytics preference locally so it can respect your choice.

## Cookies and similar storage

The static pages themselves do not require a cookie to render. If you allow analytics, Google Analytics 4 may set first-party cookies on mass.llc, including:

-   `_ga` — distinguishes unique browsers (Google’s documented default is up to two years)
-   `_ga_S0TWT8QTLL` — persists the GA4 session for this measurement ID (Google’s documented default is up to two years)

We also store your analytics choice in your browser so the site can respect it. It is not an advertising cookie. You can change or withdraw that choice using the Privacy settings control, or remove cookies and site storage in your browser.

## Who receives information

-   **MASS LLC** — inquiries you send, and analytics reports we view when you allow analytics
-   **Hostinger** — hosting and associated server logs
-   **Google LLC** — Analytics measurement if you allow it in Privacy settings
-   **Your email or phone provider** — if you email or call us

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the site and our rights. Outbound links (LinkedIn, Instagram, Threads, X, Reddit, and sources cited in articles) are third-party sites. Their collection starts if you follow the link; this policy does not cover those sites.

## Retention

-   **Email inquiries.** Kept as long as needed to respond, evaluate a possible project, or meet record-keeping and legal needs, then deleted or archived in the ordinary course of MASS email.
-   **Google Analytics.** Retained according to the retention setting on the GA4 property. We have not published a custom MASS-only warehouse of page hits.
-   **Host logs.** Retained according to Hostinger’s logging.

## Requests and updates

You may email [info@mass.llc](mailto:info@mass.llc) to ask what inquiry information we hold, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it, subject to law and records we must keep (for example an ongoing contract discussion). We will use the email address you write from to verify the request where reasonable.

Analytics and host logs are operational datasets. We can describe our practices; we may not be able to extract a complete individual profile from them.

We will change this policy when the site’s practices change—for example if we add a server-side form, a different analytics tool, or advertising tags. The effective date at the top will be updated. Continued use of the website after a posted change means the new policy applies to later visits.

This website is directed at businesses. It is not intended for children.
