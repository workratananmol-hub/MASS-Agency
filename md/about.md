## A U.S. studio in Herndon, Virginia—not limited to local work.

MASS LLC is a privately held studio, U.S.-registered and headquartered on Launders Street, Herndon, VA 20171. From that Northern Virginia base we work with clients in the Washington, D.C. area, elsewhere in the [United States](https://mass.llc/usa), and internationally—including remote engagements for organizations in [India](https://mass.llc/india) and the [UAE](https://mass.llc/uae).

The company’s public LinkedIn profile describes MASS as an AI-first development studio building AI agents, automation systems, and custom web applications for startups and scaling businesses across the US and Europe. The work itself is narrower and more concrete: websites, web applications, workflow automation, and agentic AI systems that run inside a real operation.

Founder and team

## A small, founder-led studio. The people who take the brief are the people who ship.

MASS is privately held and lists a company size of 2–10 people on LinkedIn. Delivery is not split across freelancers, junior benches, and account layers. Strategy, design, engineering, and launch stay with the core team.

The public company profile names [Anmol Ratan](https://in.linkedin.com/in/ratananmol) as part of that team. MASS’s own hiring copy states that client projects are worked on directly with the founders. There is no published org chart because the studio is not organized that way.

Responsibilities on an engagement are specific: discovery and consultation; strategy and planning; design and prototyping; development and testing; launch and handover; and, when agreed, ongoing support. The public record of that experience is the completed work below—company websites, commerce migrations, education platforms, and AI-assisted operations.

How we work with clients

1.  01

    ### Discover

    We start with the business problem, the people affected, the systems in use, and the outcome that would make the work successful.

2.  02

    ### Design

    We define the sitemap, experience, and technical shape together, so feasibility and user experience stay aligned.

3.  03

    ### Build

    The same team engineers, integrates, and tests against the agreed outcome—with progress visible before launch.

4.  04

    ### Launch

    We deploy, then transfer source files, repository access, hosting credentials, and publisher accounts. You own the work product.

5.  05

    ### Support

    Maintenance is optional. Some clients take full ownership at handover. Others keep a retainer for updates and the next slice of work.


One team. One brief. No disconnected handoffs.
