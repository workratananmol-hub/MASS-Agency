The engagement

## A commerce platform cut for performance and growth.

The situation

VastraPvt needed an online store that could support the next stage of the brand. The existing presence was not a durable commerce foundation: discovery was weak, the shopping path was harder than it should have been, and the platform would not scale cleanly with the catalog or the marketing around it.

MASS treated this as a complete storefront problem, not a theme swap. We designed the commerce experience, built it on Shopify, and later migrated the platform to WordPress when the constraint was no longer design—it was the system underneath. Redirects, technical SEO, performance, and checkout continuity were part of that move.

01 / Problem

### The brand had outgrown the store.

VastraPvt lacked a scalable e-commerce platform and an efficient online presence. Customers needed a clearer path from product discovery to purchase. The business needed a store it could actually operate as volume and content grew.

02 / Approach

### Build the journey. Then change the engine.

The Shopify storefront established the catalog, the shopping experience, and the brand’s digital standard. The WordPress migration followed when publishing, performance, or operating the store required a different foundation. MASS planned the move so search visibility and the path to purchase survived the change.

03 / Outcome

### Faster to visit. Easier to grow.

The live store is quicker, easier to find, and clearer from first product view to checkout. VastraPvt did not get a prettier skin on the same constraint. It got a commerce platform that can take the next stage of the brand.

What MASS delivered

## Better discovery. Smoother shopping.

VastraPvt is the case for migrating a store when the platform is the problem—and for designing the shopping journey before you pick a new one. If your catalog has outgrown the system underneath it, that is the brief.
