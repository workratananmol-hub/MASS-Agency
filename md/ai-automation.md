The service

## Automation starts with the process, not the model.

An AI automation system combines deterministic workflow logic with AI only where interpretation is useful. A rule routes what is known. AI can classify an email, extract a document, summarize context, draft a response, or choose among approved next steps. A person remains in control where the action carries financial, legal, customer, or operational risk.

MASS designs the whole operating path: trigger, data, validation, exception handling, approvals, writes, notifications, and audit trail. When the job needs autonomous tool use and branching decisions, the workflow can include a dedicated [AI agent](https://mass.llc/agentic-ai). When it does not, we keep the solution simpler.

## The work around the workflow is part of the workflow.

A production automation is more than a few connected nodes. MASS scopes the controls that keep it dependable after the happy-path demo is over.

### Process mapping

Triggers, decisions, systems, handoffs, exceptions, and ownership are documented before build decisions are locked.

### Integrations and credentials

Email, CRM, files, databases, APIs, and business systems need explicit permissions, stable authentication, and failure handling.

### Data and state

Deduplication, idempotency, history, and the source of truth are designed so a retry does not create a second customer, invoice, or reply.

### Human approval

High-impact actions can stop at a review queue. The system prepares the decision without silently taking authority it should not have.

### Observability and recovery

Logs, alerts, retry rules, dead-letter cases, and replay paths make failures visible and recoverable.

### Ongoing usage and support

Model, API, hosting, and monitoring costs are separated from build cost. Ongoing support is optional and scoped transparently.
