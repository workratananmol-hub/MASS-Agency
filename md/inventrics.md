The engagement

## A web presence designed to work beyond the screen.

The situation

[Inventrics](https://inventricstechnologies.com/) needed a digital front door that matched the business behind it. The public site had to explain the company, earn trust, and give a visitor a clear next step. At the same time, internal work was still carrying too much repetition—handoffs, updates, and follow-up that did not belong in email and spreadsheets.

MASS did not treat those as two projects. A modern website that ignores operations becomes another thing the team has to maintain. Automation that ignores the public experience never changes how customers actually arrive. The brief was to connect them.

01 / Problem

### Modernize the front door without leaving the work behind.

The existing presence was not doing the job a company website has to do: make the offer obvious, make the business look current, and make enquiry easy. Inside the company, processes that should have been routine were still manual. Growth would have made both problems louder.

02 / Approach

### Design the site and the workflows as one foundation.

MASS started with the audience, the offer, and the work that had to happen after a visitor acted. Then we built a responsive company website on a technical SEO foundation—crawlable structure, sensible titles, pages that can be found—and added AI-powered features and automation only where they removed real, repeatable effort.

03 / Outcome

### Clearer outside. Lighter inside.

[Inventrics](https://inventricstechnologies.com/) launched with a faster, more visible public site and workflows that take pressure off the team. Performance and search improved because the structure was built for them. Manual steps dropped because the same engagement mapped those steps instead of decorating around them.
