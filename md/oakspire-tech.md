The engagement

## Digital infrastructure shaped around real operations.

The situation

[Oakspire Tech](https://oakspiretek.vercel.app/) needed a professional digital presence that made the business easier to approach—and workflows that could keep up once a client did. A brochure site would have solved the first half of that sentence. It would have left the second half in inboxes, spreadsheets, and tribal knowledge.

MASS built a custom business website and then connected automation and AI-powered workflows to the way Oakspire already operated. The platform was not a layer of decoration over the company. It was designed around the path a client takes, and around the work the team has to do next.

01 / Problem

### Make the business easier to engage—and easier to run.

Client interactions were harder than they needed to be. Day-to-day operations depended on steps that did not scale cleanly. Oakspire needed a platform that could hold both: a credible public face, and operational flows that did not collapse when volume increased.

02 / Approach

### Start from the work, then choose the software.

MASS mapped how clients arrive, what they need to understand, and which internal steps repeat after every enquiry. The website was built as a custom professional platform. Automation and AI-powered workflows were then fitted to those operational requirements—not to a generic “AI for business” template.

03 / Outcome

### Less friction on both sides of the desk.

Clients meet a clearer, more professional digital experience. The team moves repeatable work into dependable automated flows. Efficiency improved because the platform was built around the journey, not around a stack of disconnected tools.

What MASS delivered
