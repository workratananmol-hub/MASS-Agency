MASS product 03 / Publishing · In development

Joyquill.

A MASS pipeline for turning a book brief into a print-ready PDF. It is not Amazon, not a KDP account, and not a promise that a book will be approved or will earn.

Status

Publishing pipeline in development. Not a public self-serve app.

You provide

Title direction, audience, trim size, and any text you want in the book.

Joyquill outputs

A page plan, generated interiors, and a print-ready PDF.

Amazon KDP

A destination you upload to. Joyquill does not submit or approve books.

The real workflow

From brief to PDF. Not from brief to royalty.

Joyquill is MASS’s attempt to make illustrated and structured interiors repeatable: plan the pages, generate assets, assemble, then export. A person still checks the file before anyone uploads it anywhere.

  1. Inputs

    Working title, category, audience, page count or trim, and the creative direction. Optional: manuscript text, a cover brief, and brand constraints.

  2. Page plan

    The system turns that brief into a structured outline and prompts before generation starts—so interiors are planned, not dumped.

  3. Generation and assembly

    Images and pages are produced, then laid out. Editorial checks look for duplicate pages, repeated spreads, and broken sequence—not for literary quality or market fit.

  4. Output

    A print-ready PDF sized for the intended trim. Cover files if they were in scope. You own the upload to Amazon KDP or any other printer.

Editorial checks

What we inspect. What we do not claim.

In the pipeline

  • Duplicate or near-duplicate interiors
  • Page order and blank or clipped spreads
  • Trim, margins, and PDF export for print

Not Joyquill’s job

Amazon KDP content policy, copyright of your source material, category choice, pricing, and whether the book sells. MASS is not an Amazon partner and does not operate a KDP account on your behalf in this product.

Joyquill does not guarantee publishing approval, ranking, or earnings. A finished PDF is a file. KDP is Amazon’s review.

Current availability

A MASS prototype, not a storefront.

Joyquill is not a public subscription. There is no self-serve “generate my KDP book” checkout on mass.llc. Work happens inside MASS’s development pipeline.

If you need this kind of system

That is a custom engagement.

A publishing workflow for your catalog is client work under Agentic AI or a scoped build—not an implication that Joyquill is generally available.

Ready for print is not ready for sale.

Discuss the pipeline if you produce interiors at volume. Do not treat this page as a path to Amazon income.
