What a MASS estimate includes

A price should explain what you are actually buying.

A useful estimate is not only a total. It names the scope, assumptions, exclusions, client inputs, milestones, third-party costs, and what happens after launch.

Defined outcome

Included
The business result, users, workflow, or customer journey the project is meant to improve.
Avoid
Buying a list of features with no shared definition of success.

Scope boundary

Included
Pages, product capabilities, integrations, systems, data, roles, and approval responsibilities.
Avoid
Assuming adjacent systems are included because they were mentioned in discovery.

Delivery assumptions

Included
Content, access, decision-makers, review windows, dependencies, and timing constraints.
Avoid
Quoting a calendar that depends on inputs nobody has committed to provide.

Ongoing costs

Included
Hosting, model/API usage, software subscriptions, monitoring, and optional support where applicable.
Avoid
Hiding month-two operating cost inside a low build price.
Cost drivers

The variables that move a project estimate.

The same label can describe very different systems. A five-page company site is not a portal. A fixed workflow is not an autonomous agent. A prototype is not a production multi-tenant application.

Complexity

More states, roles, templates, decisions, and exceptions mean more design, implementation, and testing.

Integrations

Each CRM, payment system, inbox, identity provider, database, or API introduces permissions, failure modes, and maintenance.

Data quality

Clean structured data is cheaper to use than inconsistent spreadsheets, conflicting documents, or records without ownership.

Risk and approvals

Work that can affect customers, money, contracts, or sensitive records needs stronger controls and evaluation.

Migration

Moving an existing site or system adds redirect mapping, data migration, parity checks, rollout planning, and rollback requirements.

Support expectations

A clean handover is different from an ongoing operating relationship with monitoring, updates, and response commitments.
