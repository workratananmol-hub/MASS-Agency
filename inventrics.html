Inventrics

A company website built as infrastructure: clear enough to earn attention, connected enough to take work off the team.

Discipline

Company website + automation

The need

Engagement outside. Efficiency inside.

What shipped

Responsive site, technical SEO, AI-assisted features, workflows

The standard

One system, not a brochure with tools bolted on

The engagement

A web presence designed to work beyond the screen.

The situation

Inventrics needed a digital front door that matched the business behind it. The public site had to explain the company, earn trust, and give a visitor a clear next step. At the same time, internal work was still carrying too much repetition—handoffs, updates, and follow-up that did not belong in email and spreadsheets.

MASS did not treat those as two projects. A modern website that ignores operations becomes another thing the team has to maintain. Automation that ignores the public experience never changes how customers actually arrive. The brief was to connect them.

01 / Problem

Modernize the front door without leaving the work behind.

The existing presence was not doing the job a company website has to do: make the offer obvious, make the business look current, and make enquiry easy. Inside the company, processes that should have been routine were still manual. Growth would have made both problems louder.

02 / Approach

Design the site and the workflows as one foundation.

MASS started with the audience, the offer, and the work that had to happen after a visitor acted. Then we built a responsive company website on a technical SEO foundation—crawlable structure, sensible titles, pages that can be found—and added AI-powered features and automation only where they removed real, repeatable effort.

03 / Outcome

Clearer outside. Lighter inside.

Inventrics launched with a faster, more visible public site and workflows that take pressure off the team. Performance and search improved because the structure was built for them. Manual steps dropped because the same engagement mapped those steps instead of decorating around them.

What MASS delivered

Specific work. No fog.

  1. 01

    Responsive company website

    A public site structured around the offer, proof, and a path to contact—readable on the phones people actually use.

  2. 02

    Technical SEO foundation

    Page structure, titles, and crawlable HTML so search engines can read the business the way a visitor should.

  3. 03

    AI-powered features

    Practical AI on the site and in the workflow, used where it shortens a real task rather than adding a gimmick.

  4. 04

    Automation workflows

    Repeatable internal steps moved out of inboxes and into dependable flows the team can run every day.

Sharper visibility. Smarter operations.

Inventrics is the argument for treating the website and the work behind it as one system. If your public site and your operations have drifted apart, that is the brief.
