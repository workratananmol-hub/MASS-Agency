    Follow light through a prism.

    Class 10 Science · Light

    One ray. Two boundaries. Watch why its direction changes.

    Silent film · On-screen captions
    Read the explanation

    A single-colour ray enters a triangular glass prism. It bends towards the normal when it enters glass from air, then away from the normal as it leaves glass for air. Its overall deviation is towards the base. The normals are perpendicular to the prism faces. This is a schematic of refraction, not dispersion.

    Class 9 English · The Fun They Had

    Follow a story contrast: a lesson alone, and the possibility of learning together.

    Silent film · On-screen captions
    Read the explanation

    In Isaac Asimov’s The Fun They Had, Margie studies alone with a mechanical teacher. A printed book introduces the idea of older schools. She imagines children learning in the same place, with a human teacher and other children to talk to. The contrast helps us discuss what shared learning could feel like. This is a paraphrased interpretation, not an extract from the story.

    Why did the voting rule matter?

    Class 9 History · The French Revolution

    From three estates to a demand for a different voice in government.

    Silent film · On-screen captions
    Read the explanation

    On 5 May 1789 the Estates-General met. Under voting by order, each of the three estates had one vote, so the two privileged estates could outvote the Third Estate two to one. The Third Estate called for voting by head: one representative, one vote. This was a demand, not a rule already adopted. Deputies declared themselves the National Assembly on 17 June. On 20 June, at the Tennis Court Oath, they pledged not to separate until a constitution was established.

    Questions

    When can I access MIMS?

    MIMS is in development. A launch date has not been announced. The first release is planned as a web app.

    Will materials be editable?

    Editable PowerPoint and teaching documents are part of the planned experience, so teachers can review and adapt the work.

    Do I need to upload textbooks?

    No. Curriculum resources are built in. Tell MIMS the teaching work you need, then review and edit the result.

    Which subjects are available first?

    The first release focuses on CBSE Classes 9–10 Mathematics in English, including Class 10 Basic and Standard. Other subjects and classes are part of the wider vision.
