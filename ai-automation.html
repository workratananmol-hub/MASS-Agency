The service

Automation starts with the process, not the model.

An AI automation system combines deterministic workflow logic with AI only where interpretation is useful. A rule routes what is known. AI can classify an email, extract a document, summarize context, draft a response, or choose among approved next steps. A person remains in control where the action carries financial, legal, customer, or operational risk.

MASS designs the whole operating path: trigger, data, validation, exception handling, approvals, writes, notifications, and audit trail. When the job needs autonomous tool use and branching decisions, the workflow can include a dedicated AI agent. When it does not, we keep the solution simpler.

Rules, AI automation, and AI agents.

The best system is not the one with the most AI. It is the one that handles the workflow reliably at the lowest sensible complexity.

Where automation earns its keep

Five workflows with measurable operational value.

01

Email intelligence

Classify inbound mail, identify intent, match attachments, extract the details that matter, and route each case to the right workflow. Human review can stay between the draft and any external reply.

02

Document processing

Read contracts, invoices, forms, applications, or rate sheets; extract structured fields; validate against rules; and send incomplete or conflicting cases to a reviewer instead of guessing.

03

CRM and revenue operations

Qualify inbound leads, enrich context, standardize notes, prepare follow-ups, update approved CRM fields, and alert a salesperson when a lead needs a human conversation.

04

Support operations

Classify tickets, retrieve approved policy, summarize customer history, suggest the resolution path, and draft a reply while keeping refunds, account changes, and sensitive actions behind approval.

05

Reporting and back office

Collect approved data sources, normalize recurring reports, flag anomalies, prepare management summaries, and keep the source trail visible for review.

What the estimate includes

The work around the workflow is part of the workflow.

A production automation is more than a few connected nodes. MASS scopes the controls that keep it dependable after the happy-path demo is over.

Process mapping

Triggers, decisions, systems, handoffs, exceptions, and ownership are documented before build decisions are locked.

Integrations and credentials

Email, CRM, files, databases, APIs, and business systems need explicit permissions, stable authentication, and failure handling.

Data and state

Deduplication, idempotency, history, and the source of truth are designed so a retry does not create a second customer, invoice, or reply.

Human approval

High-impact actions can stop at a review queue. The system prepares the decision without silently taking authority it should not have.

Observability and recovery

Logs, alerts, retry rules, dead-letter cases, and replay paths make failures visible and recoverable.

Ongoing usage and support

Model, API, hosting, and monitoring costs are separated from build cost. Ongoing support is optional and scoped transparently.
